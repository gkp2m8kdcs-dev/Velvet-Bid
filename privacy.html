Privacy Policy

Velvet Bid

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Overview

Velvet Bid ("the App") is a single-player, fully offline card game. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles and how. The short version: we collect nothing. The App has no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no telemetry of any kind.

Information We Do Not Collect

Velvet Bid does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information. Specifically, the App does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • Usage statistics, crash reports, or analytics
  • Any content you create inside the App

Data Stored on Your Device

To provide gameplay features, the App stores the following locally on your device only:

  • Your chosen player name and portrait
  • Match history, tokens, prizes, and trophy progress
  • Settings such as sound and haptic preferences

This data never leaves your device. It is not backed up to any server we operate, because we operate none. Deleting the App permanently removes all of this data.

Third-Party Services

Velvet Bid uses no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or social-media SDKs. The App makes no network requests.

Children's Privacy

Because the App collects no personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children. The App contains no real-money gambling; all tokens and prizes are virtual and carry no real-world value.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this Privacy Policy, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Any future feature that changes how data is handled would be reflected here before release.
