Velvet Bid

Step inside a velvet-draped carnival where one hundred tokens and a sharp eye decide who walks home with the crown.

About the Game

Velvet Bid is a single-player card game that revives the Italian classic Mercante in Fiera for the modern iPhone. Bid in secret against two AI traders — Vittoria the methodical Collector and Rocco the reckless Gambler — across twelve auction lots. After the hammer falls, the velvet-coated merchant arrives with six offers that can make or break your collection.

Collect matching cards across five prize tiers. Hold four of a kind to claim half the purse, six for eighty percent, and all eight for the full reward — up to one hundred points per tier.

Features

  • Secret-bid auctions on a green felt table with five suits and five prize tiers
  • A cunning merchant phase with purchases, sales, and bluffing
  • Fully offline play — no account, no login, no server, no telemetry
  • Sound cues, haptic feedback, and daily challenges
  • Nine unlockable trophies and a complete forty-card compendium
  • Virtual tokens only — no real-money gambling, no in-app purchases of value

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Velvet Bid free?
Yes. Velvet Bid is free and contains no real-money transactions.

Do I need an internet connection?
No. The game is fully offline. Your portrait, name, and match history live only on your device.

Are the tokens real money?
No. Tokens and prizes are virtual and carry no real-world value.

How do I delete my data?
All data is stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes all of it — there is nothing on our servers because we run none.
